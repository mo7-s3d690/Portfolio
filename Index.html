About

I'm a second-year Computer Science student at the Faculty of Computer and Data Science, Alexandria University, focused on data science, machine learning, and the backend systems that hold it all together. Most of what I build starts as a messy dataset or an unstructured business problem, and ends as a clean pipeline, a trained model, or a properly normalized database with a full ERD behind it.

I like the parts of data work other people skip — checking a dataset row by row for inconsistencies, designing a schema before writing a single query, documenting a pipeline so someone else could pick it up. I have a laptop, a stable connection, and a daily routine built around remote, asynchronous work — which means I'm reliable to work with even on short-notice freelance requests.
